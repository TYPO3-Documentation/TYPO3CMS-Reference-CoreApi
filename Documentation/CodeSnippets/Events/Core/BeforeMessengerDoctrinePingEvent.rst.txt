..  php:class:: BeforeMessengerDoctrinePingEvent

    Dispatched by the DoctrinePingConnectionMiddleware before a database connection
    is pinged (and transparently reconnected) ahead of a worker handling a received
    message. Listeners may skip the ping for a specific connection, for example when
    a connection must not be touched between messages.
